Lesson 1

Dilations, Scaled Copies & Slope

Watch the video, read the big ideas, play with the three explorers, then check yourself and practice until it feels easy.

The big ideas

1 · Scaled copies & scale factor

A scaled copy is a figure where every length of the original is multiplied by the same number. That number is the scale factor.

copy length = scale factor × original length
scale factor = copy lengthoriginal length
  • Scale factor > 1 → the copy is bigger.
  • Scale factor between 0 and 1 → the copy is smaller.
  • Scale factor = 1 → same size.
  • Angles stay the same. Only lengths change.
  • Adding the same amount to every side does not make a scaled copy. You must multiply.

Example: sides 3, 4, 5 with scale factor 2 → 6, 8, 10.

2 · Dilations & the center

A dilation makes a scaled copy by stretching (or shrinking) everything away from (or toward) one fixed point, the center of dilation.

  • Each image point lies on the ray from the center through the original point.
  • Its distance from the center is scale factor × the original distance.
  • The center itself doesn't move.
  • Image points get a prime mark: A → A′ ("A prime").
Center at origin: (x, y) → (k·x, k·y)

Center not at origin? Count how far the point is from the center (right/left, up/down), multiply both by k, then count that far from the center again.

Example: center (1, 1), A = (3, 2), k = 3. A is 2 right, 1 up from the center → 6 right, 3 up → A′ = (7, 4).

3 · Slope

Slope measures how steep a line is. Make a slope triangle: go across, then up or down, to get from one point on the line to another.

slope = rise (vertical change)run (horizontal change)
From (x₁, y₁) to (x₂, y₂): slope = y₂ − y₁x₂ − x₁
  • Uphill (left to right) → positive. Downhill → negative.
  • Flat (horizontal) line → slope 0. Vertical line → slope undefined.
  • All slope triangles on one line are dilations of each other (similar), so they all give the same slope.

Example: rise 4, run 6 → slope 46 = 23.

Vocabulary

Scaled copy
A figure with all lengths multiplied by the same scale factor; same shape, same angles.
Scale factor
The number you multiply every length by. Copy ÷ original.
Corresponding parts
Matching sides, angles, or points in the original and the copy (A matches A′).
Dilation
A move that makes a scaled copy using a center and a scale factor.
Center of dilation
The fixed point that everything stretches away from or shrinks toward.
Image
The new figure after a transformation (labeled with primes: A′B′C′).
Similar figures
Figures where one is a scaled copy of the other (possibly moved, turned, or flipped too).
Slope
Rise ÷ run. How much the line goes up (or down) for each 1 step to the right.
Slope triangle
A right triangle whose long side (hypotenuse) lies on the line; its legs are the rise and the run.
